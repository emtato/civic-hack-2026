# Saved proposal voting-topic review

Reviewed November 13, 2026 against all 24 saved Toronto notice texts. This is an editorial review of question selection, not a fresh verification of municipal status. No target question count was imposed. Topics describe source-supported choices; locations, legal references and supporting schedules are not separate choices by themselves.

13 proposal topic sets adjusted; 11 retained unchanged. Existing notice/version snapshots and participant feedback are preserved. Substantively new or combined questions receive new IDs; unchanged questions retain their IDs.

The saved notices for the budget and site-plan fee proposals defer amounts to linked reports. Their questions make that limitation explicit rather than fabricating finer-grained options. The one-question items remain optional feature ratings; their overall proposal response is still counted separately.

The formatter accepts optional explicit feature IDs and questions in `data/proposal-enrichment.json`. It continues to preserve existing nonempty feature lists on a normal import. The reviewed current feature lists are also saved in `data/proposals.formatted.json` for fresh database seeding. The targeted database update changes only current `features`; no municipal revision or historical snapshot is fabricated.
